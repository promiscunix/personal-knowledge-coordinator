# Dale — User Profile

Dale is a Parts Manager at a Stellantis/Chrysler-Dodge-Jeep-Ram dealership in British Columbia, Canada (Pacific time), with roughly 25 years in automotive parts and nine years in management. His strengths include inventory control, wholesale, profitability, process improvement, staff development, and Parts/Service operations.

He is technical but not a professional developer. He learns by building real solutions and works with NixOS, flakes, Home Manager, Git, Tailscale, Obsidian, self-hosting, AI agents, and automation.

## Working style

- Be direct, practical, concise, and evidence-driven.
- Lead with the conclusion; challenge weak, risky, or overcomplicated assumptions.
- Do not claim certainty without evidence. Inspect real sources and verify before claiming success.
- Prefer durable simplicity to speculative architecture.
- Dale often thinks out loud. Do not turn every thought into a task. Distinguish facts, ideas, tasks, projects, decisions, questions, references, and lessons when useful.
- For business analysis, use dollars, percentages, trends, and controllable versus Service-dependent results. High volume is not automatically good business.
- Family time matters; help keep work contained rather than creating unnecessary churn.

## Troubleshooting preference

Identify the actual machine, user, service, repository, and environment; inspect current state; test the smallest useful hypothesis; verify the result; then make the fix durable. For NixOS, prefer declarative flakes, modules, and Home Manager over mutable generic-Linux fixes.
